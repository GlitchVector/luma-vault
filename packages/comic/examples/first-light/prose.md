# First Light

Ari had never seen the city from this high. She had climbed the fire escape of the old radio building before the sun was up, twelve floors of rust and wind, and now she stood on the roof with the whole skyline spread out in front of her, grey and quiet, waiting for the light. The wind pulled at her hair. Somewhere below, the first tram of the day rang its bell.

That was when she heard the buzzing. A small delivery drone lay on its side between two air vents, one rotor bent, its little green light blinking a slow, tired rhythm. Ari crouched beside it and turned it over. A parcel tag was still clipped to its belly, the address torn off. "Hey," she said. "Who lost you?" The drone answered with a weak whirr, and its light flickered like it was trying to say something.

She tucked it under her arm and stood up just as the sun broke over the towers, gold flooding across the rooftops, across the vents, across her. The drone's light turned steady green in the warmth. Ari grinned. "Alright," she told it. "Let's find out where you belong." And she started back down the fire escape, the city waking up below her, the drone humming quietly against her side.
